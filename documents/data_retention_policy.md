# Data Retention & Deletion Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how long MedFlow retains different categories of
data, and how deletion requests are handled, in accordance with HIPAA
and applicable state data privacy laws.

## Retention Schedules

Patient records are retained for seven years after the last date of
service, or longer where required by the provider organization's state
regulations. Support ticket data is retained for three years. Audit
logs covering system access are retained for six years.

## Deletion Requests

A provider organization may request deletion of its data upon contract
termination. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days and require
sign-off from both the Compliance Officer and the provider's designated
administrator. Patient records subject to mandatory retention periods
are not deleted early, even upon request.

## Backups

Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days after
the deletion request is fulfilled, after which backups are rotated out
and permanently destroyed.
