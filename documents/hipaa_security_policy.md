# HIPAA & Data Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how MedFlow protects protected health information
(PHI) in accordance with HIPAA. It applies to all employees and any
provider staff with access to the MedFlow platform.

## Access Control

Access to PHI is granted on a least-privilege basis. All system access,
for both employees and provider staff, must be requested through the
access management system and approved by a manager. Access reviews are
conducted quarterly.

## Password Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters, include a mix of letters,
numbers, and symbols, and be rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor
authentication is required for all accounts with access to PHI.

## Security Training During Onboarding

Both new employees and newly onboarded provider staff must complete
HIPAA security awareness training before receiving system access. This
training is a mandatory step in both the employee onboarding process and
the provider onboarding process, and is tracked separately by People
Operations and Provider Support respectively.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected data breach or unauthorized access must be reported to the
Security team within one hour of discovery.
