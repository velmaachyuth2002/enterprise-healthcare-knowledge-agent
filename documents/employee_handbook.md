# MedFlow Health Systems — Employee Handbook

## About MedFlow

MedFlow Health Systems builds software used by hospitals and clinics to
manage provider onboarding, scheduling, patient portals, and compliance
reporting. This handbook applies to all MedFlow employees.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity, respect patient and provider
confidentiality at all times, and disclose any conflict of interest to
their manager. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be
tolerated.

## New Employee Onboarding

New hires complete a two-week onboarding process managed by People
Operations. This includes account provisioning (email, Slack, VPN), a
benefits enrollment session, assignment of an onboarding buddy, and
role-specific training modules. Employees must complete HIPAA security
awareness training before any system access is granted. Onboarding is
considered complete once the new hire's manager signs off in the HR
system.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year, plus 10 company
holidays. PTO requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least
two weeks in advance where possible.
