# Security Incident Response Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook describes the steps MedFlow employees follow when a
suspected security incident - a data breach, unauthorized access, or
lost or stolen equipment - is discovered. It expands on the brief
reporting requirement in the HIPAA & Data Security Policy.

## Step 1: Report Immediately

Report any suspected incident to the Security team within one hour of
discovery, using the incident hotline or the #security-incidents Slack
channel. Do not wait to confirm the incident is real before reporting -
false alarms are expected and preferred over delayed reports.

## Step 2: Contain

The Security team assesses the incident and, if needed, revokes access
credentials, isolates affected systems, or disables integrations
suspected of being compromised. Employees should not attempt to
investigate or remediate the incident on their own.

## Step 3: Notify Affected Parties

If an incident is confirmed to involve PHI, the Compliance Officer
determines whether provider organizations and, where required by law,
affected individuals must be notified. Notification decisions and
timelines follow HIPAA breach notification requirements.

## Step 4: Post-Incident Review

Every confirmed incident is followed by a post-incident review within
two weeks, documenting root cause and any process changes needed to
prevent recurrence.
