# Remote Work & Equipment Policy

## Eligibility

Most MedFlow roles are eligible for full-time remote work. Roles that
require on-site provider training or in-person compliance audits are
the primary exceptions, and are noted as such in the job description.

## Equipment

MedFlow issues a company laptop to every employee, which must be used
for all work involving PHI or provider data. Personal devices may not
be used to access the MedFlow platform under any circumstances.

## Home Network Security

Employees working with PHI must connect through the company VPN at all
times, use a password-protected home network, and may not work from
public Wi-Fi without the VPN active.

## Equipment Return

Company equipment must be returned within five business days of an
employee's last day. IT issues a prepaid shipping label for remote
employees; equipment is wiped remotely if not returned within 30 days.
