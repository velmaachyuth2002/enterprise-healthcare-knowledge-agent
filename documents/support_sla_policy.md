# Customer Support SLA & Escalation Policy

## Response Time Targets

Urgent-priority tickets receive an initial response within one hour.
High-priority tickets are responded to within four business hours.
Medium and low-priority tickets are responded to within one and two
business days respectively.

## When to Escalate a Ticket

A ticket should be escalated to urgent priority when it affects a
provider's ability to submit claims, blocks patient scheduling, or
involves a suspected security or data privacy issue. Escalation
requires a brief written reason and must be approved by a Support
Manager before the ticket's priority actually changes.

## After-Hours Support

Urgent tickets are monitored 24/7 by the on-call Provider Support
Engineer. Non-urgent tickets submitted outside business hours are
picked up at the start of the next business day.

## Ticket Ownership

Every ticket has a single owning Provider Support Engineer, even if
multiple team members contribute to resolving it. Ownership transfers
are logged so a ticket's history stays traceable.
